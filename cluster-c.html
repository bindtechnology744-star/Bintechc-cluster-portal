Welcome to Cluster C

Cluster C brings together three technical-vocational programs: Automotive Technology, Welding Technology, and Mechanical Technology. These programs focus on developing students' technical knowledge, practical skills, and readiness for future employment. Through classroom instruction, hands-on training, and the guidance of instructors, the department aims to prepare students for opportunities in technical and industrial fields.
